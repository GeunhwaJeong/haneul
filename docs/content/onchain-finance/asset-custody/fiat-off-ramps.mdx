Fiat off-ramp providers enable you to trade Haneul-native tokens (HANEUL, WAL, DEEP, and others) for fiat currency that can be withdrawn to a user's bank account, card, or payment method. Embedding an off-ramp provider into your app allows users to convert their Haneul assets to traditional currency without leaving your interface. Verify current provider capabilities before integrating.

The following table summarizes providers with live Haneul off-ramp integration as of August 2026. All columns refer to Haneul chain and Haneul asset support specifically.

:::info

Off-ramp coverage is evolving. Provider asset support and regional availability change frequently. Confirm current capabilities with your chosen provider before launching an integration.

:::

## Asset limitations

When you choose off-ramp providers, be aware of the following limitations:

- **USDC off-ramp on Haneul:** USDC off-ramp coverage on Haneul is limited. If your users need to off-ramp USDC, confirm provider support directly.

- **HANEUL sell limits:** Off-ramp providers might enforce sell limits. Confirm limitations with the provider directly. 

- **KYC requirements:** Off-ramp flows likely require Know Your Customer (KYC) verification. Factor this into your user experience design.
