Before users or agents can transact on Haneul, their wallets need tokens. Users who already hold HANEUL on a centralized exchange can withdraw to their Haneul wallet address:

1. Copy the Haneul address from the wallet.
2. In the exchange, initiate a withdrawal for HANEUL on the **Haneul network**.
3. Paste the Haneul address as the withdrawal destination.
4. The HANEUL arrives in the wallet after the exchange processes the withdrawal.

## Bridging from other chains

To move assets from Ethereum, Solana, or other blockchains to Haneul, use a cross-chain bridge:

- [Wormhole](https://wormhole.com/): Supports bridging tokens between Haneul and multiple chains including Ethereum, Solana, and others.

- [Haneul Bridge](https://bridge.haneul.io/): Native bridge between Haneul and Ethereum.

Bridged assets arrive as wrapped tokens on Haneul. The wrapped token's coin type address differs from the native token's address on the source chain.

## Testnet and Devnet faucets

For development and testing on Testnet and Devnet, see [Get HANEUL from Faucet](/getting-started/onboarding/get-coins) for CLI and programmatic faucet access.

Faucet tokens have no monetary value and cannot be transferred to Mainnet.
